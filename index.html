About Albany Dentist

Albany Dentist is a general and cosmetic dental practice on Madison Avenue in Albany, serving families across the city.

We cover everyday dentistry, such as exams, cleanings and fillings, as well as more specialized services including pediatric care, orthodontics, crowns, root canal therapy and dental implants.
